### 2.3. Hand-flow lexicon — Strategy B (primary marks + casual-variant common-law pool)

The 8-beat lexicon below is the canonical, proprietary terminology for the eight discrete events of a Yellow Jacket Tour hand. The order matches real-golf semantics: **action → state** alternation, with the preflop deal+bet collapsed into the Tea Box (the same way a real tee box collapses tee+drive into one beat). Each primary mark is being **used in a trademark sense** starting from the 2026-05-02 inventory date.

This adopts **Strategy B** from the brand discussion (see `LEXICON.md`): one canonical primary mark per beat is the registration target, and a documented pool of **casual variants** is used in marketing and flavor copy to build common-law rights without separate paid filings.

| # | Primary mark (file) | Type | Poker phase | Casual variants (common-law only) |
|---|---------------------|------|-------------|------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Tea Box** | combined state + action | Preflop deal + opening pot + first betting round | The Tee, The Drive, The Open, The First Sting, The Tee-Off |
| 2 | **The Fairway** | state | Flop deal (3 community cards) | The Reveal, The Lay, First Bloom, The Spread |
| 3 | **The Lay-Up** | action | Flop betting event | The Iron, The Second, The Press, The Set |
| 4 | **The Hazard** | state | Turn deal (4th community card) | The Lie, The Rough, The Bend, The Trap |
| 5 | **The Approach** | action | Turn betting event | The Run-Up, The Line, The Set-Up, The Pitch |
| 6 | **The Green** | state | River deal (5th community card) | The Pin, The Flag, In Close, On Pin |
| 7 | **The Putt** | action | River betting event | The Strike, The Read, The Sink, Final Sting |
| 8 | **The Cup** | resolution state | Showdown | The Drop, The Hole-Out, The Bottom, Ball In |

**On the corrected order (vs. earlier draft):** an earlier draft listed Drive at slot 3 (flop bet). Real golf order has the drive as the *first* shot off the tee, with the fairway as the landing — meaning Drive should precede Fairway, not follow it. The corrected resolution: collapse "the drive" semantically into the Tea Box (where the tee shot actually happens), use **Lay-Up** as the proprietary name for the second shot (the flop bet), and document **Drive** as a casual variant for Tea Box (so players can still say "I went big on the drive" meaning their preflop raise).

**Trademark posture for the 8-stage lexicon:**

- All eight terms are used consistently in `RULES.md`, `index.html`, and all marketing materials as proprietary brand terms, not as generic descriptors.
- Each term is paired with a parenthetical familiar reference on first use in any document (e.g., `Drive (the flop bet)`) so that the reader learns the term as a brand name attached to a familiar concept. This is the "Whopper model": the brand term replaces the generic, but the generic is acknowledged so readers don't get lost.
- Several of these terms (Drive, Hazard, Putt, Cup) are common golf words on their own and would face descriptiveness refusals if filed at TEAS as standalone marks today. Their trademark strength comes from **the proprietary system they name** (the specific eight-event hand structure of Yellow Jacket Tour) and the **stylistic/visual treatment** they receive in the product. When ready to file, the recommended path is:
  1. File **the 8-mark suite as a "family of marks"** rather than 8 separate filings — a family argument lets the weaker individual marks ride on the distinctiveness of the system as a whole.
  2. File the visual/stylized form (logo treatment) of each, not the bare word, when stylized assets are produced.
  3. Combined estimated cost when filed: ~$2,000 (8 × $250 TEAS Plus) plus possible attorney fee for the family-of-marks brief. Defer until the brand is in commerce and the stylized treatments exist.
- In the meantime, **document and use consistently** to build common-law rights. Common-law trademark rights are real and enforceable in the US, just narrower in geography than registered marks.

## 4. Game-Mechanic and Design Assets (Patent Candidates / Defensive Publication Candidates)

These are the protectable design elements. Their patent eligibility under *Alice v. CLS Bank* is uncertain; the recommended posture is **dual-track**: brief a patent attorney while simultaneously preparing the defensive publication (see `DEFENSIVE_PUBLICATION.md`) to lock in prior-art protection if patents are not pursued.

| Mechanic | Description | Protection mode |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Honey-Stroke scoring law** | Per-hole bounded golf score (−5..+2) derived from showdown hand class, summed with normalized honey credit, forming a single hybrid score per hole | Patent claim candidate (combination claim); defensive publication backup |
| **Round-divisor normalization** | Net honey divided by integer divisor {1, 4, 9, 36} for {1, 9, 18, 72}-hole rounds, applied exactly once at round end | Patent claim candidate (technical method); defensive publication backup |
| **Agreed-total wagering primitive** | Pot is most recently accepted proposal; bets/raises propose new total; calls accept (distinct from matched-contribution wagering) | Patent claim candidate (game-rule mechanic — Alice-vulnerable); defensive publication strongly recommended |
| **Tied-pot carry-forward** | Tied holes roll the agreed pot forward to the next hole | Defensive publication; weak patent posture alone |
| **Dual-variant loss-rule system** | Yellow Jacket = decisive loser posts +1 fixed bogey; Bumblebee = decisive loser posts own hand-class score. Same engine, same wagering, same caps | Patent claim candidate (combination); defensive publication backup |
| **Adaptive Bogey-Loss percentile rule** (lowball) | Loser at ≥70th percentile posts own hand; otherwise posts +1 | Defensive publication |
| **Hybrid Schema C bucket mapping** (lowball) | Percentile-aligned scorecard buckets labelled with conventional hand-class names | Defensive publication |
| **Survival-cushion + handicap late-reg system** | Late entrants seeded at `leader + cushion × multiplier` rather than at par | Patent claim candidate; defensive publication backup |
| **Stroke-cap progressive unlock** | 25/50/75/100% honey-pot caps unlocked by community-card street | Defensive publication |
| **Variant Calibration Mode** (audit primitive) | Bootstrap-CI-based parameter sweep over a Cartesian grid of variant parameters | Defensive publication; potentially patent-eligible as a technical improvement to game-design tooling |
| **Hand-class to golf-score map** (16-row Hold'em table) | Categorical mapping of poker hand classes to bounded golf scores | Copyright (the specific table); defensive publication for the methodology |
